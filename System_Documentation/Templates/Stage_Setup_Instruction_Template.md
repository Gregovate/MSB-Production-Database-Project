## Template Authoring Rules

Delete this section before publishing the field instruction.

- Make a working copy/source for the specific Stage or Scene before replacing template placeholders.
- Do not manually type step numbers when the authoring system provides numbered-list formatting.
- Use document styles/headings consistently rather than manually reproducing font sizes and spacing.
- Use indentation controls rather than tabs for nested steps.
- Keep Setup-specific images in the approved Setup-local image location defined by the Stage documentation workflow.
- Place images next to the step they explain.
- Use the approved reusable public MSB logo asset rather than creating per-Stage logo copies.
- Do not add database, QR, repository, or engineering implementation detail unless the field volunteer needs it to perform the task.
- Keep the published document focused on the physical setup task.
- Archive superseded field instructions through the established Stage document-alignment procedure rather than deleting them.
- Verify the final published PDF/rendered document before marking the instruction CURRENT.
