MSB LOR Preview Update
======================

Use this before starting work on MSB Light-O-Rama sequences.

1. CLOSE LIGHT-O-RAMA
   Make sure the Light-O-Rama Sequencer is closed.

2. DOUBLE-CLICK
   "Update your PC Previews to LOR Master Files"

3. WAIT FOR THE CHECK TO FINISH
   The updater automatically finds the latest accepted LOR2DB production
   reconciliation and the correct approved versioned master-preview folder.
   You do not select a version or folder.

4. REVIEW THE RESULTS
   The window will show:
   - accepted LOR2DB Run
   - approved master source
   - controlled previews already current
   - controlled previews that will be added
   - controlled previews that will be replaced
   - personal/unmanaged previews that will be preserved

5. APPROVE THE UPDATE
   Confirm Light-O-Rama is closed.
   If controlled MSB previews will be replaced, acknowledge the warning.
   Click "Update PC Previews".

6. REOPEN LIGHT-O-RAMA
   After the updater reports success, reopen Light-O-Rama and continue working.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Do NOT manually import the full MSB preview library.
- Do NOT copy another programmer's LORPreviews.xml.
- Do NOT choose a "Database Previews Vx.x.x" folder yourself.
- Personal/imported/store previews are preserved.
- The updater gets the approved master location from the latest COMPLETED
  LOR2DB reconciliation report.
- If the approved source is missing or does not match the LOR2DB evidence,
  the updater stops rather than guessing.

If the updater reports BLOCKED or FAILED, stop and contact the MSB database administrator.
